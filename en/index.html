Chapter 14 · The Heart and Circulation第 14 章

14.2Four chambers, two circuits四个腔、两条路

心脏分左右两半，中间有隔膜（septum）隔开，左右的血不相混。每一半上面是心房（atrium），负责收血；下面是心室（ventricle），负责往外泵。

The heart has left and right halves, separated by the septum so their blood does not mix. Each half has an upper atrium that receives blood and a lower ventricle that pumps it out.

血流一圈要过心脏两次。右心把全身回来的缺氧血送进肺，叫肺循环（pulmonary circulation）；左心把肺里回来的富氧血送往全身，叫体循环（systemic circulation）。

Blood passes through the heart twice per lap. The right heart sends oxygen-poor blood from the body to the lungs (pulmonary circulation); the left heart sends oxygen-rich blood from the lungs to the body (systemic circulation).

Questions常见问题

What do I need?

An iPhone or iPad running iOS / iPadOS 26 or later. The app's interface is in Simplified Chinese; the book text is in Chinese and English, with an English-only reading mode.

Do I need to be online or sign up?

No. Everything is inside the app and works offline, with no ads. Your progress, bookmarks and notes stay on your device and in your own iCloud — we can't see them, and we collect no data.

Can this replace seeing a doctor?

No. It's a popular-science book, not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Its numbers and conclusions come from published authoritative sources, with references at the end of each chapter and in Appendix B; AI-generated illustrations are labeled in their captions.

I got a new phone. Do I keep the Full Book?

Yes. Sign in with the same Apple ID and tap Settings → 完整版 (Full Book) → 恢复购买 (Restore Purchases). Members of your Family Sharing group can use it too.
